### What's new since v0.1.0-alpha

- **Your tags spread to look-alikes.** The app learns what your own tags look like ("Kermit", a
  friend's name) and adds them to stickers that look the same, so they're found too.
- **Tag suggestions.** Those learned tags, grouped by tag for review: untick the wrong ones and add
  the tag to the rest in one tap. Open it from the line under the sticker count, or from Smart
  search.
- **Tags from your searches.** Send the same sticker twice for the same short search and that
  search becomes one of its tags, so it's found by those words everywhere.
- **More picture tags.** About 150 new labels: expressions, gestures, Israeli food and holidays,
  chat reactions, and popular sticker characters. Existing stickers are re-tagged quickly, without
  running the picture model again.
- **Search learns from you.** Stickers you send after a search rank higher next time, and keyword
  results are ordered by how well they match. About → Clear search history forgets it.
- **Clearer tagging progress.** A progress bar and time left while picture tags run, and why it's
  paused (waiting for the charger, low battery, starting soon).
- **Fixes.** Sending stickers (unique file names, cleaned up after use), deleting face data while
  it's being scanned, sticker details after saving, a single multi-word tag, and more. The app is
  also smaller and a little faster (minified), checked on an emulator before every release.
